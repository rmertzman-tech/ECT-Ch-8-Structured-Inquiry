Who Is Authoring the Route?

Audit the route. Does this tool expand agency, or does it capture it?

How to use this

Live class: complete ENTER individually, compare in pairs/groups, and open each UNLOCK only when the instructor reaches it.

Online/self-paced: work individually and open the three UNLOCKS in order. At COMPARE, construct a strong alternative map rather than waiting for a group.

Target: about 35–45 minutes of active inquiry distributed across a 60–75 minute class, or about 30–40 minutes self-paced.

1 · ENTER — First Contact

The AI-Written Email

DeShawn is struggling in a class. He wants to email his professor to ask for help, but he feels embarrassed and does not know how to begin. He opens an AI writing tool and asks it to draft the email. For now, you know only that the tool gives him a polished starting point.

Before discussion or chapter explanation, make your own first map.

Claim ceiling: your first reaction is data for inquiry, not a final verdict. Preserve it before later information changes the map.

2 · COMPARE — Difference Harvest

Expand the map; do not vote on it

Comparison rule: difference is useful evidence about what the first map made salient. The goal is not immediate consensus.
Live-class board prompt: Share one item under Different / Missing / Still unresolved . Online students can use the same three headings in a discussion post if assigned.
Instructor + chapter
3 · UNLOCK — Teach Into the Case

Open each instrument when the chapter reaches it

These are not a mini-lecture replacing the chapter. They are short instruments for testing the map you already made.

UNLOCK 1 — Tool support vs agency capture
Instructor pause 1 · self-paced students open in order

The AI draft says: “I take full responsibility for my lack of discipline. I have failed to manage my time appropriately.” It sounds polished, but it does not sound like DeShawn and may not describe his situation accurately.

Instrument: a tool can support agency by helping someone notice, understand, communicate, decide, and repair. It can capture agency when it replaces contact, judgment, authorship, responsibility, feedback, or repair.

UNLOCK 2 — Authorship and data are not the person
Instructor pause 2 · self-paced students open in order

DeShawn has been working late shifts and caring for a family member. He still has responsibilities, but the phrase “lack of discipline” compresses his situation into a generic explanation.

Instrument: authorship means being able to understand, test, revise, and stand behind what one submits. Data or a generated description may show something real without exhausting the person or the context.

UNLOCK 3 — Appeal and correction are part of the route
Instructor pause 3 · self-paced students open in order

Tools make mistakes and can hide context. A responsible route therefore includes opportunities to inspect, correct, explain, revise, or appeal an output rather than treating it as a verdict.

Instrument: the question is not simply whether AI was used. It is whether the final route preserves contact, judgment, authorship, responsibility, feedback, and repair.

Transfer — The Resume Screener

A company uses an automated screener to rank job applicants. The system quickly identifies candidates whose résumés resemble past successful hires. An applicant with a two-year employment gap is ranked low. The system does not know the gap involved caregiving, and the applicant has no clear way to learn why the score was low or correct the record.
